# Changelog

Changes to Pageframe, newest first, in the [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) format. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org): releases are tagged `vX.Y.Z` and nightly betas `vX.Y.Z-beta.YYYYMMDD.N`. Before `1.0.0`, a minor bump means a breaking change. Entries come from commit subjects on `main` and move under a version heading when it's released. Blitz's own history is in its [repository](https://github.com/DioxusLabs/blitz).
